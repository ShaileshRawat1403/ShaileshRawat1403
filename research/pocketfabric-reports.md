# PocketFabric research and PocketNode validation

Two reports by Shailesh Rawat, Independent Researcher, published on 31 October 2026. Both are openly available under CC BY 4.0. They have not undergone journal peer review.

## Research case study

**A Second Life Under Constraints: An Experimental Study of Repurposing an Android Smartphone as Computing Infrastructure**

[Read the paper and download its source package](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23836212)

A rooted OnePlus 9, kept on stock Android 14 and its vendor kernel, hosted personal services, coding-agent terminals, a GPU research harness and encrypted storage. The study documents kernel compatibility, isolation, Android policy, supervision, power and numerical-correctness constraints.

The evidence record includes a pre-study on a second phone, 28 completed experiments, 6 refused or interrupted runs, 8 incidents and 10 offline analyses. The reported cold GPU comparison used one run per configuration; the study does not establish energy savings, population-level performance or long-term availability.

## Functional validation

**PocketNode: Functional Validation of a Personal Smartphone Application Node**

[Read the report and download its supporting evidence](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23836251)

An assessment of exact source revisions and bounded read-only observations on 31 October 2026. It records automated test outcomes, live service checks and unresolved findings involving backup verification, launcher verification, a development dependency and tailnet access. Later local console fixes are distinguished from the installed release.

The report supports inspection of the checks performed. It does not certify long-term availability, full disaster recovery or security for multiple untrusted users.
